# XFlow Hero artwork

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool and simplified through an edit of the initial generated concept. The final asset is `xflow-hero.webp`, 1672 × 941 pixels. The user-directed style is a softly blurred post background with the simplest geometric lines.

## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: a wide, understated Hero background image for the XFlow website.
Primary request: Make the artwork much simpler and less deliberate. The user wants only the simplest geometric lines over a softly blurred post background.
Canvas: 16:9 landscape, opaque charcoal background.
Background: a quiet, heavily softened impression of a social timeline. Only three or four faint post silhouettes: small blurred avatar circles and short blurred horizontal text strokes, barely visible in dark neutral grey. No readable text, no logos, no literal screenshot, no browser controls.
Foreground: a very sparse arrangement of hairline straight geometric strokes, in muted grey. Two or three short horizontal lines and one short vertical line, lightly offset and loosely aligned. A small, incomplete rectangular outline may connect two strokes. Keep the geometry restrained and incidental, like a few subtle editorial registration marks. No dominant central symbol, no X shape, no flow diagram, no large frame enclosing the entire image.
Composition: flat, calm, airy, lots of uncluttered negative space, only a few fine strokes. The blurred posts are background texture and should not compete for attention. The result must look intentionally quiet when placed below the website heading and buttons, including at mobile width.
Style: minimal 2D graphic, neutral black, charcoal and grey only. Low contrast background, slightly clearer lines. Flat surfaces with no texture or physical materials.
Constraints: replace the previous sculptural aperture completely. No 3D, no cut metal or paper planes, no object, no gate, no metaphor, no spotlight, no glow, no gradients, no glass, no decorative icon collection, no bright white central post, no loud shapes, no text, no watermark.
Output: one exceptionally simple and restrained image: a softly blurred post background with a few fine geometric lines.
